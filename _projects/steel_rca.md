**Feasibility study for a European producer of flat steel products.**

The producer recorded a large number of types of visual defects with an automated machine vision setup, and asked which root causes led to which defects.

The analysis found that the defects were most strongly correlated with a single central process parameter, but without any view on cause and effect: do the defects lead operators to adjust that parameter, or does the parameter cause the defects? The study exposed the main pain points of classical machine learning for root cause analysis, namely **causal directionality** and **strongly correlated data**, and showed the limits of data-driven modelling with explainability but without a causal basis or the integration of external knowledge.
