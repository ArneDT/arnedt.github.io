**Service project for a producer of bakery and dough products.**

Starting from an industrial dataset of lab tests, we focused on root cause analysis and causal relations behind quality deviations in a bakery process.

The offline causal analysis of the lab data produced concrete, actionable insights: the results were used to guide **sensor placement in the design of new production lines**. The case shows that an offline causal approach already generates real, tangible value.
