**Machine Vision for Quality Control**

The TETRA project MV4QC aimed to accelerate the implementation of machine vision for quality control in companies. It collected and organised existing knowledge and new technology in the field and passed it on in a pragmatic way, helping companies work out which hardware and software they need for their applications. The target group was broad: large and small companies, especially non-R&D-intensive ones, across many sectors, because quality control matters in every industry.

As project researcher, I worked on inline machine vision for quality control and process optimisation. Use cases included visual defect detection in wood plating, population estimation in fish processing, and fat and moisture content estimation in bakery products. The bakery work led to a paper at [IEEE INDIN 2023](https://doi.org/10.1109/INDIN51400.2023.10217889).
