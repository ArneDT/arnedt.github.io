SmartMalting aims to automate and self-regulate the malting process. The project maps the process using innovative sensor technologies, AI-driven data models and a formalisation of the domain expertise of experienced malters.

The challenge lies in the natural variability of the raw material and the complex interactions within the process. The research focuses on multi-sensor integration, hybrid AI modelling and semantic knowledge representation.

First results show that combining hybrid AI modelling with semantic knowledge representation yields useful insights into the process. The next steps build on this towards an intelligent decision-support system for malt production.
