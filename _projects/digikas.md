**Digitalisation in greenhouse tomato cultivation**, together with Inagro and tomato growers.

Tomatoes are traded on characteristics such as fruit size, variety and Brix index. Every day, growers and their advisors make decisions on irrigation, ventilation, screen control and lighting to find the right balance between yield, disease pressure and energy costs. These decisions are often made on intuition, based on many data points from plant sensors, climate sensors and energy prices, and the sector is cautious about automated control.

In its first two years, the project achieved promising results in root cause analysis of diseases and production, and in sensor selection. The next phase moves towards hybrid modelling, combining data-driven models with plant-physiological models for optimisation.
