I am a PhD researcher at [DTAI](https://dtai.cs.kuleuven.be/), KU Leuven Campus Bruges, supervised by Mathias Verbeke. I work on **multi-objective optimization with graphical causal models under uncertainty**, to support decision-making in manufacturing.

I also run The Ultimate Factory, our Industry 4.0 innovation lab, and previously worked as a data and software engineer at voestalpine. More in my [CV](cv/).
